Privacy policy

AI Hub Remote for iPhone and iPad. Last updated 3 October 2026.

In short

AI Hub Remote collects no data for its maker or for anyone else. It has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. What you do in the app goes only to the PC you paired it with.

Where your data goes

The app shows the pages of AI Hub, which runs on your own Windows or Mac PC. Your recordings, photos, documents and text go only to that PC, through that PC's own secure address (its owner's Cloudflare account). They are stored on that PC, by AI Hub, under the control of whoever runs it.

The first time you pair, your PC's sign-in may ask for your email address to send you a one-time code. That sign-in belongs to the PC's owner, not to us.

On the phone, the app keeps the list of PCs you paired, and a recording only until your PC has all of it.

What the app asks to use

  • Microphone: to record meetings, and to hear you in Interpreter and Assistant.
  • Camera: to scan the pairing code on your PC, and to photograph papers you add.
  • Photos: to add the photos you choose to your work.
  • Notifications: to tell you when a call paused a recording, when your PC's sign-in needs you, or when the phone is too full to go on recording.

Each is used only when you ask for it, and you can turn it off in the iPhone's Settings.

Removing your data

To remove a PC from the phone, open App → PCs, swipe its row to the left and tap Forget. Deleting the app removes everything it kept on the phone. What is stored on a PC is removed in AI Hub on that PC.

Children

The app is not directed at children, and it collects no data from anyone.
