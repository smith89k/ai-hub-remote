AI Hub Remote

The AI Hub on your PC, on your iPhone and iPad.

What it is

AI Hub is a desktop app for Windows and Mac for interpreting, meetings and writing. AI Hub Remote opens its tools from your PC on your iPhone or iPad, wherever you are: the Work Desk, Interpreter, Assistant, Meeting, Watch and your Workspaces.

You can record a meeting on the phone, even with the screen locked. The recording goes to your PC, which writes the transcript and the report.

What you need

  • AI Hub on a Windows or Mac PC, with phone access set up (on the PC: Administration → Phone access).
  • An iPhone or iPad with iOS 17 or later.

Pair your phone

  1. On the PC, open Administration → Phone access → Pair a phone. A code appears.
  2. On the phone, open AI Hub and tap Connect, then point the camera at the code.
  3. The first time, your PC's sign-in sends a code to your email. Enter it. The Work Desk opens.

No camera? On the PC, press Download beside the code, put the picture on the phone, and tap Choose a Photo on the scan screen. The code works for 10 minutes.

More than one PC: in the app, open App → PCs → Add a PC and scan the other PC's code.

No PC yet?

Tap Try the Demo on the first screen. It shows each tool with made-up sample content.

If something goes wrong

The app can't reach your PC
Check that the PC is on and AI Hub is open, then tap Reconnect. A meeting you record meanwhile is kept on the phone and goes to the PC once it can be reached.
"This phone isn't paired any more"
The phone was removed on the PC. Pair it again.
"Update AI Hub on your PC to use this app."
Install the newest AI Hub on the PC.
The sign-in asks for an email code again
Enter the code your PC's sign-in sends. It is part of your PC's own security.
